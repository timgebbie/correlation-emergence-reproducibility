# Coupling-only theory/estimator conformity — `v1.7.5`

Status: accepted on 2026-08-20

## Decision

The reduced symmetric linear-SDE reference is `recovered`. The accepted
Angstmann--Gebbie thick-boundary coupling experiment is
`invalid_experiment`: it does not provide the independently required positive
local response-rate bracket. The stochastic thick-boundary Epps curve and the
combined no-refit prediction are intentionally not executed.

This result is not a poor fit of an Epps curve. It is a fail-fast diagnosis
made before that curve is allowed to exist. No replacement coupling is
selected, and no source, thickness, selector or combined parameter is fitted.

## Reduced reference and estimand separation

For response rate `kappa`, aggregation scale `Delta` and `x=kappa*Delta`, the
registered response is the covariance normalized by `C*Delta`:

\[
F(x)=1-\frac{1-e^{-x}}{x}.
\]

For the same closed symmetric two-price SDE, the exact finite-scale return
correlation is instead

\[
\rho_{\mathrm{SDE}}(x)
=\frac{x-(1-e^{-x})}{x+(1-e^{-x})}.
\]

Figure 20 and the CSV retain both objects separately. Treating `F` as a
correlation additionally needs the approximately linear marginal-variance
closure stated in the paper.

The reduced experiment uses 32 paths, an exact stationary OU-spread
transition, a 0.5-second uniform step, a 200-second warm-up and a 10,000-second
analysis interval. Centre and spread normal arrays are generated by the gate
script from separate declared seeds, made sample-orthogonal, and passed into a
response module that owns no random generator. Delete-one-path jackknife
standard errors accompany the pooled component-sum estimators.

## Thick-boundary local-response audit

The accepted coupling density is

\[
\ell_\varepsilon(y,z)
=\gamma zq(y)\frac12\left[1+\tanh\left(\frac{yz}{\varepsilon}\right)\right].
\]

At small spread its expansion is

\[
\ell_\varepsilon(y,z)
=\frac{\gamma}{2}zq(y)
 +\frac{\gamma}{2\varepsilon}z^2yq(y)
 -\frac{\gamma}{6\varepsilon^3}z^4y^3q(y)+O(z^6).
\]

The only first-order term is proportional to the uncoupled source. In the
linear stationary density problem, that mode rescales the reaction front but
does not translate its zero crossing. A first-moment projection of the source
to an assumed price velocity is therefore an additional closure; it is not
realized automatically by the accepted density recurrence.

The numerical audit constructs eight signed symmetric translation-mode
perturbations on the existing 201-point uniform grid. It uses zero innovations,
identity clocks and paired uncoupled controls, then measures both exponential
spread relaxation and the coupling-induced local drift over the first 40
seconds. Boundaries remain unique and interior, the uncoupled controls are
stable, and the controlled displacement remainder is negligible.

| `gamma` | Exponential rate per model unit | Local-drift rate per model unit |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.641915 | -0.00000132 | -0.00000407 |
| 2 | -0.00001279 | -0.00003956 |
| 10 | -0.00031995 | -0.00098960 |
| 20 | -0.00128092 | -0.00396699 |
| 40 | -0.00514176 | -0.01600756 |

The required rate is `2.5` per model-time unit. The scan has no positive rate
and cannot bracket that target, so interpolation in `gamma` is forbidden.

correction design, `v1.7.7` for corrected coupling recovery and initially

the receiving-book sign convention, selector limit and translation-mode
