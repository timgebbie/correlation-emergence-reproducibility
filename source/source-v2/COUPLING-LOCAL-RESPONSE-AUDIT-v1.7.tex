\section*{Local-response audit of the regularised thick-boundary coupling}

The accepted thick-boundary implementation evaluates
\[
  \ell_\varepsilon(y,z)
  =\gamma zq(y)W_\varepsilon(y,z),
  \qquad
  W_\varepsilon(y,z)
  =\frac12\left[1+\tanh\left(\frac{yz}{\varepsilon}\right)\right].
\]
At fixed $y$ and $\varepsilon>0$, its small-spread expansion is
\[
  \ell_\varepsilon(y,z)
  =\frac{\gamma}{2}zq(y)
   +\frac{\gamma}{2\varepsilon}z^2yq(y)
   -\frac{\gamma}{6\varepsilon^3}z^4y^3q(y)
   +O(z^6).
\]
The only term linear in $z$ is therefore proportional to the uncoupled source
$q(y)$.  For a stationary density satisfying a linear equation with source
$q$, a perturbation proportional to $q$ changes the amplitude of the
stationary front to first order but leaves its zero crossing fixed.  The
first shape component capable of displacing the zero crossing enters here at
order $z^2$, not at order $z$.

Consequently, the mean-reverting boundary law
\[
  \frac{d}{du}z(u)=-\kappa z(u)+o(z)
\]
does not follow from the implemented density recurrence merely by assigning
the raw first moment of $\ell_\varepsilon$ to a translation velocity.  That
assignment is an additional closure, and the present numerical zero crossing
does not realise it.  Symmetric translation-mode perturbations confirm this
directly: over the registered positive-$\gamma$ scan, both the paired local
drift and paired exponential response rates are non-positive and do not
bracket the required $\kappa=2.5$ per model-time unit.

The reduced symmetric two-price SDE remains internally consistent.  If
$x=\kappa\Delta$, its covariance divided by $C\Delta$ is
\[
  F(x)=1-\frac{1-e^{-x}}{x},
\]
whereas its exact finite-scale return correlation is
\[
  \rho_{\mathrm{SDE}}(x)
  =\frac{x-(1-e^{-x})}{x+(1-e^{-x})}.
\]
Thus $F$ is the normalized covariance response.  Treating it as a correlation
additionally requires the approximately linear marginal-variance closure
already stated in the paper.

No replacement thick-boundary coupling is selected at this gate.  A correction
must separately resolve the receiving-book sign convention, the side-selector
limit and the translation-mode projection, then demonstrate a positive local
rate before any Epps curve is fitted or any combined no-refit prediction is
attempted.
