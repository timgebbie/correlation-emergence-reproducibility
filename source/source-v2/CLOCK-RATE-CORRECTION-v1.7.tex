\section*{Clock-rate correction for the equal-rate previous-refresh benchmark}

Let the two books have independent Poisson refresh processes with the same
rate
\[
  \lambda_1^{\rm clk}=\lambda_2^{\rm clk}=\lambda^{\rm clk},
\]
and let $\gamma_j(t)$ be the latest refresh time of book $j$ not exceeding
$t$.  Sampling the operational-time price by
$P^{(j)}(t)=p^{(j)}(\gamma_j(t))$ gives the exact equal-rate attenuation
derived for the previous-refresh estimator in Equation~29 of T\'{o}th,
T\'{o}th and Kert\'{e}sz (2007, arXiv:0704.3798):
\[
  \mathcal A_{12}^{\rm Pois}(\Delta t)
  =1-\frac{1-e^{-\lambda^{\rm clk}\Delta t}}
           {\lambda^{\rm clk}\Delta t},
  \qquad
  \rho_{\Delta t}^{\rm sub}
  =\rho_u\,\mathcal A_{12}^{\rm Pois}(\Delta t).
\]
Thus $\lambda^{\rm clk}$ is the refresh rate of each identically
distributed book.  The minimum of the two independent forward waiting times
is separately exponential with rate
$\lambda_1^{\rm clk}+\lambda_2^{\rm clk}=2\lambda^{\rm clk}$, but that
minimum-wait identity does not replace $\lambda^{\rm clk}$ by
$2\lambda^{\rm clk}$ in the previous-refresh interval-overlap result.

For unequal Poisson rates and for general inverse clocks, the numerical
conformity route uses the exact realised operational-interval intersection,
not the equal-rate closed form and not the pooled minimum-wait rate.  The
earlier pooled-rate envelope is retained only as a rejected diagnostic.  This
correction changes the theory-to-simulation rate map; it does not tune the
simulation to the displayed curve.
