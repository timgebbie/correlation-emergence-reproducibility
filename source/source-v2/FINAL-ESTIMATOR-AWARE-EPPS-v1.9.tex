\section*{Final estimator-aware theory--simulation integration}

The final comparison retains the accepted clock and coupling parameters and
evolves both order-density books on the uniform operational-time grid. The two
book-specific previous-refresh clocks are applied only after the operational
paths are complete. Realised waiting intervals therefore do not enter the
state recurrence, and calendar-time interpolation is not used.

Figure~26 places the clock-only, corrected coupling-only and combined results
on one common linear aggregation scale. The first two panels compare the
accepted simulations with their respective analytical responses. The third
panel compares the combined no-refit holdout with both
\[
  F(\lambda\Delta)F(\kappa\Delta)
\]
and the exact conditional finite-grid, finite-step moment calculation on the
operational indices selected by the same realised clocks.

The same-clock estimator-aware curve reduces the combined normalized-
covariance RMSE from $0.066963$ for the leading-order product to $0.039719$.
Its standardized RMSE is $0.455132$, with pointwise normal 95\% coverage at
all 20 registered lags. No clock rate, coupling rate, boundary normalization,
time map or tolerance is changed. The conditional curve is consequently a
finite-estimator refinement, not a fitted replacement for the paper's
leading-order separability argument.
