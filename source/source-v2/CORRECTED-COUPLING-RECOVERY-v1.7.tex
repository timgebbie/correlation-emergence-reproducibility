\section*{Theory consequence of the corrected coupling recovery}

The v1.7.5 audit showed that the fixed-$\varepsilon$ regularised source
\[
  \ell_\varepsilon^{(j,k)}
  =\gamma_{jk}z_{jk}q_j(y)
  \frac{1}{2}\left[1+\tanh\left(\frac{yz_{jk}}{\varepsilon}\right)\right]
\]
does not generate the positive reaction-boundary relaxation rate assumed by
the registered thick-front experiment.  This does not invalidate the
conditional adjoint calculation in the paper.  It shows that the step from the
raw first spatial moment of this source to the effective coefficient
$\kappa_{jk}$ is a closure assumption, rather than a property established for
the complete numerical recurrence.

The corrected numerical source implements the paper's translation mode
directly:
\[
  v_j(x,u)=-\partial_x\varphi^{(j)}(x,u),
  \qquad
  \ell_T^{(j,k)}(x,u)
  =-\kappa_{jk}z_{jk}(u)v_j(x,u).
\]
For the normalized adjoint response mode $\psi_j$,
\[
  \dot p_j
  =\frac{\langle\psi_j,\ell_T^{(j,k)}\rangle}
  {\langle\psi_j,v_j\rangle}
  =-\kappa_{jk}z_{jk}.
\]
Thus $\kappa_{jk}$ is an explicit ordered response-rate parameter; it is not a
reinterpretation of the legacy source amplitude $\gamma_{jk}$.  The thickness
of the forcing is inherited from the current receiving-book front through its
spatial derivative, and the density is frozen at the immutable pre-step
state.

With $\kappa_{12}=\kappa_{21}=1.25$ per model-time unit, eight signed
deterministic perturbations recover exponential rates with maximum relative
error $0.00612$ and local-drift rates with maximum relative error $0.000646$.
The pair centre is preserved to numerical precision, and all boundaries remain
unique and interior.  A three-grid sequence reduces the exponential-rate error
from $0.0251$ to $0.00143$.

The stochastic comparison keeps two distinct estimands.  The normalized
cross-covariance response is compared with
\[
  F(\kappa\Delta)
  =1-\frac{1-e^{-\kappa\Delta}}{\kappa\Delta},
\]
using an independently frozen pair-centre variance-growth rate as its scale.
The holdout RMSE is $0.01670$.  The realised return correlation is compared
with the distinct exact symmetric closed-SDE expression and has holdout RMSE
$0.00877$.  Both comparisons have full pointwise normal 95\% coverage under
the declared path-group jackknife.

The paper's reduced spread response and its normalized covariance envelope are
therefore supported by the corrected implementation.  The manuscript should
not claim that the original regularised $\tanh$ source necessarily realizes
the same $\kappa_{jk}$ in the full thick-front recurrence.  A later manuscript
revision should retain that source as a phenomenological or weak-moment
representation, identify its source-to-rate step as conditional, and present
the translation-mode source as the direct numerical realization of the
reduced boundary dynamics.  The source-v1 manuscript remains unchanged until
this gate is externally accepted.

All dynamics in this recovery are evolved on the uniform operational grid.
The identity clock is used for component isolation; no nonuniform state update,
calendar interpolation or subordination is present.  The combined clock and
coupling prediction remains a separate v1.7.8 holdout test.
