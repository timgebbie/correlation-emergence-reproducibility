No empirical or external data are required for v1.0.0.

All scientific curves are deterministic evaluations of equations in the frozen
target paper and the documented finite-grid diagnostic.
Machine-readable generated data are written to outputs/.

Future v2.0.0 simulation curves must use the semantic fields in
outputs/epps-overlay-v1.csv and record numerical resolution, alignment, domain,
uncertainty, parameter profile, and software version.
